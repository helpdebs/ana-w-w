Step 3 · Final text

Write it up

Step 4 · Self-review

Be your own editor

  1. Read version 1 out loud once.
  2. Check it with the review lens.
  3. Write exactly 2 improvement points: what you noticed + how you'll fix it.
  4. Rewrite the text applying both points. This is version 2, the base for your talk.

Version 1

Green = expression found

Review lens

  • Are the 5 expressions in a natural context, or just dropped in?
  • Is the structure clear: intro, ideas, opinion?
  • Do I repeat the same words (very, good, thing, people, nice)?
  • Are my connectors varied (however, on top of that, that's why, even so)?
  • Are the verb tenses consistent: past for what happened, present for my opinion?
  • Is there at least one fact or number from my research?

Step 5 · Prep time

Build your cue card

  1. Start the timer: 10:00
  2. Fill in the cue card: 5 lines, maximum 6 words per line.
  3. Choose 1 opening and 1 closing signpost.
  4. Say your opening out loud 2 times.
  5. Talk from the cue card. Your text stays closed during the talk.

Step 6 · Presentation

Your stage

  1. Start the stopwatch and begin.
  2. Speak for 3–5 minutes.
  3. Use all 5 expressions. Tap each one as you say it.
  4. Look only at the cue card.
  5. Finish with 1 clear opinion.
